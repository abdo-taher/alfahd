## Overview: How FAQs Capture Search Real Estate

FAQ content targets four distinct SERP features:

1. **Featured Snippets** — Google pulls a direct answer from the page to display in position 0. FAQ-style content with clear question H2s and concise answer paragraphs is the most reliable way to win featured snippets.
2. **People Also Ask (PAA) Boxes** — Google's expandable Q&A boxes appear on almost every informational query. FAQ pages with properly structured content feed directly into PAA results.
3. **Voice Search** — Smart speakers and voice assistants source answers directly from structured FAQ content. As Saudi Arabia's smart device adoption grows, FAQ voice search traffic will increase.
4. **AI Overviews (Google)** — Google's AI Overview cites structured content with clear Q&A format. FAQ pages with FAQPage Schema are among the most-cited content types in AI Overviews.

**Implementation note:** Every FAQ page and major service page must include FAQPage Schema JSON-LD. See the Schema markup section at the end of this document.

## Schema Markup Implementation: FAQPage JSON-LD

### For Service Pages (embed within service page head or body)

Apply FAQPage Schema to any page containing FAQ sections. Example for the Aluminum Works pillar page:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What aluminum works do you provide in Riyadh?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Al-Fahad Contracting provides a complete range of aluminum works in Riyadh including aluminum windows, aluminum doors, aluminum facades, curtain wall systems, aluminum cladding, custom aluminum systems, and aluminum skylights. We serve commercial, residential, government, and industrial projects across Riyadh and Saudi Arabia."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "How much do aluminum works cost in Saudi Arabia?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Aluminum works costs in Saudi Arabia vary by project scale and specification. Standard aluminum windows cost approximately SAR 800–1,500 per sqm installed. Aluminum facade systems range from SAR 1,500–4,000 per sqm. Curtain wall systems range from SAR 3,000–8,000+ per sqm depending on system complexity. Request a free quote for accurate project-specific pricing."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Is aluminum suitable for Saudi Arabia's hot climate?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Yes. Aluminum is highly suitable for Saudi Arabia's climate. It is naturally corrosion-resistant, does not rust or degrade in extreme heat, and powder-coated aluminum maintains its finish for 20+ years in desert conditions. For energy efficiency, thermally broken aluminum profiles with double or triple glazing significantly reduce heat transfer."
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

Any blog article that answers a specific question should include a targeted 2–3 question FAQPage Schema block focused on the primary query the article addresses.
