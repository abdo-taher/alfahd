### Search Intent Analysis

**Primary buyer types:**
- **Real estate developers (P1):** MOFU/BOFU — searching for "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض" or "شركة ألمنيوم معتمدة" to shortlist contractors for project packages
- **General contractors (P1):** BOFU — searching for "توريد وتركيب ألمنيوم" to subcontract aluminum works on active projects
- **Government procurement (P1):** BOFU — searching for "مقاول ألمنيوم معتمد حكومي" during pre-qualification or tender evaluation
- **Villa owners (P2):** MOFU — searching for "نوافذ ألمنيوم فلل الرياض" for residential renovation budgeting
- **Architects and consultants (P2):** MOFU — searching for "أنظمة ألمنيوم معمارية" to specify systems in project documents

**Intent pattern:** 70% of searches are Arabic; primary intent is commercial (comparison + shortlisting) at MOFU transitioning to transactional (quote request) at BOFU. Decision cycles are 2–8 weeks for small residential projects and 3–12 months for commercial/government projects.

### Recommended Content Sections

1. **Hero / Above-the-fold** — H1 + eyebrow label + primary CTA (اطلب عرض سعر) + project photo background
2. **Service Overview Paragraph** — 200–300 words introducing Al-Fahad's aluminum capabilities, experience, and scope
3. **Sub-Services Grid** — 7 card grid (one per sub-service) with icon, Arabic name, English name, 1-line description, link to sub-service page
4. **Why Choose Al-Fahad** — Stats strip: 25+ سنة خبرة / 300+ مشروع / شهادات جودة / تسليم في الوقت المحدد; then 3–4 differentiator bullets
5. **Industries We Serve** — Grid of 6–8 industry tiles (Villas, Residential, Commercial, Government, Hospitality, Industrial) each with icon and link to industry page
6. **Featured Projects Gallery** — 3–6 project cards with image, project name, location, year, and link to project page
7. **Client Testimonials** — 2–3 testimonials from developers/contractors with company name and role
8. **FAQ Block** — 5–8 Q&A in accordion format with FAQPage Schema
9. **Cost Guide CTA** — Inline callout: "Wondering about pricing? → Aluminum Facade Cost Guide Riyadh"
10. **Primary CTA Section** — Full-width banner: headline + اطلب عرض سعر button + استشارة مجانية ghost button
11. **Certifications Strip** — ISO / SASO / Vision 2030 partner logos with alt text

**Recommended word count:** 2,000–3,000 words (including FAQ block). Do not pad with low-value filler; every paragraph must add topical authority or conversion value.
