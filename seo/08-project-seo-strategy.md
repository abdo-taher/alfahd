# 08 — Project SEO Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the SEO framework for individual project pages — URL structure, content template, keyword targeting, Schema markup, metadata strategy, and internal linking rules. Al-Fahad's 300+ projects are an irreplaceable SEO asset that no competitor can replicate.

---

## Why Project Pages Are SEO Assets

Most Saudi contractors publish a portfolio gallery with thumbnail images and no text. This creates an enormous content gap that Al-Fahad can exploit:

1. **Each project page targets a unique keyword combination** — `{service} + {location} + {year}` — e.g., "aluminum facades Riyadh 2023 commercial tower". No competitor has content targeting these specific combos.
2. **Project pages build Topical Authority** — 50+ well-written project pages tell Google that Al-Fahad has deep, diverse, real-world experience across every service type, industry, and location. This signals directly compound the authority of Core_Service pillar pages.
3. **Project pages convert late-stage buyers** — Searchers who find a project in their neighborhood or sector are in the final due-diligence phase. A compelling project page with challenge → solution → results narrative is the highest-converting content type for B2B contractor selection.
4. **Long-tail query monopoly** — "glass curtain wall Al-Olaya Riyadh residential tower" has near-zero competition. Al-Fahad can rank #1 for 200+ such queries simultaneously.
5. **Internal link equity distribution** — Each project page links to its parent service page and relevant location/industry pages, systematically boosting those commercial pages' authority.

## URL Structure Template

```
/en/projects/{service}-{location}-{year}/
/ar/mashari3/{service}-{location}-{year}/
```

**Examples:**

| Project Type | English URL | Arabic URL |
|---|---|---|
| Aluminum facades, Al-Olaya, 2023 | `/en/projects/aluminum-facades-al-olaya-2023/` | `/ar/mashari3/wajhat-aluminium-al-3ulayya-2023/` |
| Glass curtain wall, North Riyadh, 2022 | `/en/projects/glass-curtain-wall-north-riyadh-2022/` | `/ar/mashari3/kirtun-wul-shamal-riyadh-2022/` |
| Steel warehouse, Industrial Riyadh, 2024 | `/en/projects/steel-warehouse-riyadh-2024/` | `/ar/mashari3/hanjar-ma3dani-riyadh-2024/` |

**URL rules:**
- Service slug: `aluminum-facades`, `glass-curtain-wall`, `steel-warehouse`, `aluminum-windows`, etc.
- Location slug: use district, neighborhood, or city name in transliterated form
- Year: 4-digit year of project completion
- Maximum 6 URL segments after locale prefix
- No client names in URL (legal and confidentiality reasons)

## SEO-Optimized Content Template

Each project page should follow this structure. Minimum content: 800 words.

### Section 1: Project Hero

- Full-width or 2/3-width feature image (before/after if available, or best completion photo)
- Project title: `{Service Type} — {Location}, Riyadh {Year}` in H1
- Eyebrow label: service category (e.g., "أعمال الألمنيوم" / "Aluminum Works")
- Key project stats strip: Project Scale (sqm or units), Completion Year, Location, Client Type (not necessarily named)

### Section 2: Project Overview (100–200 words)

- 2–3 sentences summarizing: what was delivered, for whom (client type), where, and when
- Include primary keyword naturally: e.g., "This commercial aluminum facade project in North Riyadh..."
- Second paragraph: any notable engineering or design challenges

### Section 3: Client Challenge (150–250 words)

- What was the client's problem/requirement?
- What constraints existed: timeline, budget, custom specification, extreme Saudi climate conditions?
- Why was this project complex or technically demanding?

*This section signals E-E-A-T (Experience) — it proves Al-Fahad has real-world expertise, not just product brochures.*

### Section 4: Solution Approach (200–350 words)

- How did Al-Fahad solve the challenge?
- Which specific products/systems were selected and why? (e.g., "We specified a thermally broken aluminum curtain wall system with 6mm tempered outer pane to address the extreme Riyadh summer heat load")
- Engineering decisions made and their rationale
- Any innovative techniques or custom fabrication involved

*This section drives topical authority — it demonstrates technical depth that generic competitor content cannot match.*

### Section 5: Materials & Specifications

- Structured list or table:
  - Material types used
  - Brand/grade/specification
  - Quantities (approximate)
  - Finishes and coatings
  - Certifications of materials

*Tables are ideal for this section — they signal structured, factual content to AI search engines.*

### Section 6: Execution Process (150–250 words)

- Timeline: from mobilization to handover
- Team size and structure
- Key construction milestones
- Any notable site challenges (access, phased construction, occupied building)

### Section 7: Project Gallery

- 6–12 high-quality photos
- Each photo: descriptive file name (`aluminum-facade-north-riyadh-office-tower-2023.jpg`)
- Each photo: alt text with primary keyword + descriptive detail (e.g., "Aluminum curtain wall facade installed on a 12-story commercial tower in North Riyadh, completed 2023")
- Caption: brief description of what the photo shows

### Section 8: Measurable Results (100–200 words)

- Quantify outcomes where possible: sqm installed, floors covered, completion time vs. target, client satisfaction metrics
- Example: "Delivered 3,200 sqm of aluminum curtain wall façade, completed 2 weeks ahead of schedule on a 14-floor commercial tower in Al-Olaya, Riyadh"

*Measurable results give AI search engines quotable, factual snippets — increasing citation probability in AI Overviews and AI-generated answers.*

### Section 9: Internal Link Block (In-Page)

At the bottom of each project page, place a structured "Related" block:
- "Explore our [Service Name] services" → link to Core_Service page
- "More projects in [Location]" → link to location page
- "View similar projects" → 2–3 related project page cards
- CTA: "Have a similar project? → Request a Quote"

## Keyword Targeting Patterns

Each project page targets a cluster of 3–5 keywords using this formula:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| `{service} + {city/district} + {year}` | aluminum facades north Riyadh 2023 |
| `{service} + {client type} + {city}` | glass curtain wall commercial tower Riyadh |
| `{specific product} + {location}` | curtain wall Al-Olaya |
| `{service} project + {city}` | aluminum facade project Riyadh |
| Arabic: `مشروع {خدمة} {موقع}` | مشروع واجهات ألمنيوم شمال الرياض |

**Never target head terms** (e.g., "best aluminum company") on project pages — those belong on pillar pages. Project pages should target ultra-specific long-tail combinations.

## Metadata Strategy

**Title template:** `[Project Name / Service Type] — [Location], Riyadh [Year] | Al-Fahad`
**Example EN title:** `Aluminum Curtain Wall Facade — North Riyadh Commercial Tower 2023 | Al-Fahad`
**Example AR title:** `واجهات كيرتن وول ألمنيوم — برج تجاري شمال الرياض 2023 | مؤسسة الفهد`

**Description template:** `[Scope summary]. [1 key technical detail]. [Location + year]. Delivered by Al-Fahad Contracting — 25+ years of facade excellence in Riyadh.`
**Example EN desc:** `3,200 sqm aluminum curtain wall facade for a 14-floor commercial tower in North Riyadh, completed 2023. Thermally broken system, powder-coated finish. By Al-Fahad Contracting.`

## Schema Markup: CreativeWork

Use `CreativeWork` Schema type for all project pages. This is the correct type for portfolio/project showcases.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "CreativeWork",
  "name": "Aluminum Curtain Wall Facade — North Riyadh Commercial Tower",
  "description": "3,200 sqm aluminum and glass curtain wall facade system installed on a 14-floor commercial office tower in North Riyadh, completed 2023.",
  "creator": {
    "@type": "Organization",
    "name": "Al-Fahad Contracting Company",
    "alternateName": "مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات"
  },
  "dateCreated": "2023",
  "locationCreated": {
    "@type": "Place",
    "addressLocality": "North Riyadh",
    "addressCountry": "SA"
  },
  "about": {
    "@type": "Service",
    "name": "Aluminum Curtain Wall Systems"
  }
}
```

## Internal Linking Rules for Project Pages

### FROM project page, links TO:

| Link Target | Anchor Text (EN) | Anchor Text (AR) | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent Core_Service page | "Aluminum Works" / "Glass Works" / "Steel Works" | "أعمال الألمنيوم" / "هندسة الواجهات الزجاجية" / "الهياكل المعدنية" | **Required** — 1 link to parent pillar |
| Most relevant sub-service page | "Curtain Wall Systems" | "أنظمة الكيرتن وول" | **Required** — 1 link to most specific sub-service |
| Location page | "Projects in North Riyadh" | "مشاريعنا في شمال الرياض" | **Required** — 1 link to matching location |
| Industry page | "Commercial Building Projects" | "مشاريع المباني التجارية" | **Recommended** — if project type is clear |
| Related project pages | "Similar Projects" | "مشاريع مشابهة" | **Recommended** — 2–3 project cards |
| Request Quote | "Have a similar project?" | "لديك مشروع مشابه؟" | **Required** — conversion CTA |

### TO project pages, links FROM:

- Core_Service pillar pages (project gallery section)
- Sub-service pages (project examples)
- Location pages (projects in this area)
- Industry pages (sector-specific project examples)
- Case study hub page
- Blog articles referencing the project type

## How Project Pages Build Topical Authority

The mechanism is straightforward: Google's Helpful Content and E-E-A-T systems reward publishers that demonstrate **direct experience with the subjects they cover**. A project page describing the exact engineering decisions made on a real curtain wall installation in a real Riyadh tower is the highest-quality signal of experience that a contractor website can produce.

Specifically:

1. **Keyword diversity:** 50 project pages collectively rank for 50 unique long-tail queries. This forces Google to categorize Al-Fahad as an expert in the entire aluminum/glass/steel contracting domain, not just a contractor who claims expertise.

2. **Topical depth reinforcement:** Every project page that mentions "thermally broken aluminum curtain wall system" as a keyword phrase adds another co-citation link between Al-Fahad and that technical concept in Google's entity graph.

3. **Internal link equity flow:** Each project page links to its parent service pillar. If 10 project pages all link to `/en/services/aluminum/` with variations of the anchor "Aluminum Works," this systematically boosts the pillar page's authority on that term — even before any external backlinks are built.

4. **Freshness signals:** Regularly publishing new project pages (1–2 per month) signals that Al-Fahad is an active, growing business — a freshness signal that correlates with improved rankings for commercial-intent queries.
