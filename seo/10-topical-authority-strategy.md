## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the topic cluster architecture that positions Al-Fahad as the authoritative entity for aluminum, glass, and steel works in Saudi Arabia. Topical authority is the mechanism by which a website's ranking power compounds over time.

---

## Introduction: Why Topical Authority Matters

Topical authority is Google's confidence that a publisher comprehensively covers a given subject area. It is built by publishing multiple interconnected pieces of content that collectively cover every dimension of a topic — not just the commercial "hire me" pages, but also informational guides, comparison articles, FAQ content, cost guides, and educational resources.

When Google determines that Al-Fahad has the most comprehensive, credible, and interconnected content on aluminum facade contracting in Saudi Arabia, it rewards the site with ranking boosts across **all** keywords in that topic space — including competitive head terms like "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض" that would otherwise require years of backlink building.

**The mechanism:** A topic cluster consists of:
1. **Pillar Page** — The definitive, comprehensive guide on the main topic (e.g., "Aluminum Works in Riyadh")
2. **Cluster Pages** — Dedicated pages on sub-topics (e.g., individual sub-service pages, cost pages, industry pages)
3. **Supporting Articles** — Blog/informational content covering related questions and use cases

All cluster pages and supporting articles link back to the pillar page. The pillar page links out to cluster pages. This creates a web of topical co-citation that tells Google the entire cluster belongs to the same authoritative source.

## How Topical Authority Impacts Commercial Keyword Rankings

The compound effect works as follows:

1. **Months 1–3:** Sub-service pages and FAQ content begin ranking for long-tail queries. Google starts associating Al-Fahad's domain with aluminum/glass/steel topics.
2. **Months 3–6:** As cluster pages accumulate ranking history, Google's confidence in the domain's topical depth increases. The pillar pages begin ranking for mid-tail commercial queries ("aluminum works Riyadh", "glass facade company Riyadh").
3. **Months 6–12:** Full topical authority established across all three clusters. Pillar pages rank in top 5–10 for competitive head terms. New content ranks faster due to pre-established topical authority.
4. **Months 12–24:** Compound effect kicks in — each new piece of content ranks faster and higher than earlier content because the domain is recognized as the authoritative source. Competitive head terms like "أفضل مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض" become reachable.
