## Strategy Overview and Guiding Principles

Al-Fahad starts with a near-zero backlink profile. The link building strategy follows a "foundations first" approach — building a stable, credible base of local and industry citations before pursuing authority links, and always prioritizing links that drive both SEO value and referral traffic.

**Guiding principles:**
1. **Quality over quantity.** One DA 50 link from a relevant Saudi construction publication outperforms 50 links from irrelevant DA 10 directories.
2. **Relevance is non-negotiable.** Every link must come from a site in the construction, architecture, real estate, or Saudi business space.
3. **Earn, don't buy.** All links must be earned through genuine content value, relationships, or directory listings — never purchased or exchanged in schemes.
4. **Diversify anchor text.** Branded anchors (مؤسسة الفهد / Al-Fahad), URL anchors, and natural descriptive anchors. Never over-optimize with exact-match keyword anchors (e.g., do not build 20 links with anchor "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض").
5. **Local-first.** Saudi-hosted or Saudi-focused domains pass the strongest local authority signal for KSA SERP rankings.

## Black-Hat Exclusions

The following link acquisition tactics are explicitly prohibited. They violate Google Webmaster Guidelines and will result in manual or algorithmic penalties that can take 12–18 months to recover from:

| Prohibited Tactic | Why It's Prohibited |
|---|---|
| **Paid link placement** (paying sites to add a link) | Direct Google policy violation; undetectable short-term but penalized by Link Spam updates |
| **Private Blog Networks (PBNs)** | Networks of sites created solely to pass link equity; detectable by Google and penalized |
| **Link exchanges** ("I'll link to you if you link to me") | Reciprocal link schemes are a spam signal, especially at scale |
| **Automated link building** (software-generated links) | Creates patterns that trigger spam detection |
| **Comment spam** (posting links in blog comments) | Low quality, easily detected, no SEO benefit |
| **Forum/social spam** (adding links to discussion threads without genuine contribution) | Violates platform terms of service and Google guidelines |
| **Keyword-stuffed anchor text campaigns** (building 20+ links with identical exact-match anchor "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض") | Anchor text manipulation signals; triggers Penguin-type signals |
| **Hidden links** | Direct cloaking violation |
| **Article spinning** (machine-generated variation content for mass submission) | Low-quality signal; content spam |

**Note to content team:** If any agency or freelancer offers "100 links for SAR X,000" packages, these are PBN or spam links without exception. Reject all such proposals.
