# 09 — Case Study SEO Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the framework for SEO-optimized case study pages. Case studies differ from project pages in depth, intent, and SEO value — they target decision-stage buyers performing due diligence and signal the highest level of demonstrated expertise to Google's quality raters.

---

## How Case Studies Differ from Project Pages

| Dimension | Project Page | Case Study Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Portfolio showcasing | Deep expertise proof |
| **Word count** | 800–1,200 words | 1,500–3,000 words |
| **Keyword target** | `service + location + year` | `service + outcome + client challenge` |
| **Buyer stage** | MOFU — building a shortlist | BOFU — final due diligence |
| **Schema type** | CreativeWork | Article (or CreativeWork with Article overlay) |
| **Conversion role** | Awareness → shortlisting | Shortlisting → RFQ/consultation |
| **E-E-A-T signal** | Experience (we did this work) | Expertise + Experience (we solved this specific problem expertly) |
| **SEO value** | Long-tail project queries | Competitive commercial queries ("best aluminum contractor") |
| **Frequency** | Every project (50+) | Selected deep-dive projects (15–25 total) |

Case studies are prioritized for projects that:
- Have a compelling challenge-solution narrative (not just "we installed X")
- Feature a measurable, quantifiable result
- Represent a client type Al-Fahad wants to attract more of
- Demonstrate a technical capability competitors cannot credibly claim

---

## Case Study Framework: 5-Section Structure

### Section 1: Client Challenge (200–400 words)

This section establishes the problem. Write from the client's perspective:

- **Who was the client?** (type, not necessarily named — e.g., "a Tier-1 real estate developer in North Riyadh")
- **What was the project scope?** (building type, scale, timeline)
- **What specific problem or constraint needed solving?** Examples:
  - Extreme time pressure (project handover date was fixed)
  - Complex architectural specification (irregular facade geometry, bespoke aluminum profiles)
  - Extreme budget constraint with non-negotiable quality requirements
  - Saudi climate challenge (high UV exposure, extreme temperature cycles, sandstorm resistance)
  - Phased construction on occupied building
  - Government compliance requirements (Saudi Building Code, SASO specifications)

**SEO purpose:** This section establishes the query context. Buyers searching "aluminum contractor for commercial tower tight deadline" will find content that directly addresses their situation.

**Writing guidance:** Do not write marketing language. Write like an engineering project brief. Specific technical details (dimensions, temperatures, load specs) are signals of genuine experience.

---

### Section 2: Solution Approach (300–500 words)

This section is the core of the case study and the highest E-E-A-T signal:

- **What solution did Al-Fahad propose?** (not just "we installed aluminum" — but which system, which grade, which configuration, and WHY)
- **Engineering decisions made:** material selection rationale, system configuration choices, structural considerations
- **How was the solution validated?** (shop drawings, structural calculations, mock-up testing, thermal modelling)
- **Coordination challenges:** working with other trades, site access constraints, phasing requirements

**Key phrases to include naturally:**
- System specifications (e.g., "70mm thermally broken aluminum profile system")
- Standards referenced (e.g., AAMA 2605 powder coat specification, SASO building code compliance)
- Engineering tools used (e.g., "wind load analysis per Saudi Wind Load Code")
- Custom fabrication details

**SEO purpose:** Dense technical content on specific product/system terms creates topical authority on specialty queries like "thermally broken curtain wall Riyadh" and "AAMA 2605 aluminum contractor Saudi Arabia" — queries that only serious buyers search.

---

### Section 3: Materials & Specifications (structured list or table, 200–300 words)

Present in a structured table format (signals factual, quotable content to AI search):

| Component | Specification | Source/Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Aluminum alloy | 6063-T5 extrusion | ASTM B221 |
| Surface finish | PVDF powder coat, 80 microns | AAMA 2605 |
| Glass | 6mm tempered outer / 16mm argon gap / 6mm tempered inner | EN 12150 |
| Glazing system | Structural silicone, two-component | Dow 993 |
| Thermal break | Polyamide PA66 GF25 | ISO 14024 |
| Framing system | 70mm face, stick system curtain wall | CWCT TN17 |

This table format is specifically optimized for:
- Google featured snippets (tables are frequently pulled into answer boxes)
- AI search citation (structured factual content is cited at high rates by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity)
- Architect/consultant trust building (technical buyers verify specs)

---

### Section 4: Execution Process (200–350 words)

Describe how Al-Fahad delivered the project:

- **Pre-construction:** mobilization, site survey, shop drawing approval timeline, material procurement
- **On-site execution:** installation sequence, team size and structure, daily/weekly milestones
- **Quality assurance:** inspection protocols, water-tightness testing, final commissioning
- **Challenges overcome on-site:** specific obstacles that arose and how they were solved
- **Timeline:** planned vs. actual, reasons for any variance, how delays were mitigated

**Key metric to include:** "Installed [X] sqm per day on average" — this is a quotable performance benchmark that differentiates professional contractors from small operators.

---

### Section 5: Measurable Results (150–300 words)

Quantify every outcome that can be measured:

- **Scope:** "Delivered 4,800 sqm of aluminum curtain wall across 18 floors"
- **Timeline:** "Completed 3 weeks ahead of schedule"
- **Budget:** "Delivered within the approved ±5% budget tolerance" (do not disclose exact figures unless client permits)
- **Quality:** "Zero water infiltration incidents recorded in 12-month post-handover period"
- **Client satisfaction:** include a verbatim quote if client permission is obtained
- **Business impact:** "The completed facade contributed to the building achieving full commercial tenancy within 6 months of handover"

**SEO purpose:** Quantified outcomes are the most powerful elements for:
1. AI search citation (AI systems prefer citing specific, factual, attributed statements)
2. Conversion (buyers at the decision stage respond to proof of results)
3. Topical authority (specific numbers signal experience — a 4,800 sqm installation is a distinct data point Google cannot generate through inference)

## Keyword Targeting Strategy

Case studies target a different keyword tier than project pages — they go after evidence-based commercial queries:

| Pattern | Example (English) | Example (Arabic) |
|---|---|---|
| `service + challenge type` | aluminum facade complex geometry Riyadh | واجهة ألمنيوم مواصفات معقدة الرياض |
| `service + client type + city` | curtain wall contractor hotel Riyadh | مقاول كيرتن وول فنادق الرياض |
| `service + performance outcome` | aluminum facade delivered on time tight schedule | واجهات ألمنيوم تسليم في الوقت بالرياض |
| `service + industry + scale` | steel warehouse 5000 sqm Riyadh | هانجر معدني 5000 متر الرياض |
| `how we solved type queries` | how to install curtain wall Saudi climate | كيفية تركيب الكيرتن وول في المناخ السعودي |

**Important:** Each case study must have 1 primary keyword in its H1, URL, and opening paragraph. The remaining keyword targets appear naturally in the body text.

## Internal Linking Patterns

### FROM case study, links TO:

| Link Target | Anchor Text | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Parent Core_Service pillar | "Aluminum Works" / "Glass Works" / "Steel Works" | **Required** |
| Most relevant sub-service page | "Curtain Wall Systems" | **Required** |
| Location page | "Projects in North Riyadh" | **Required** |
| Industry page | "Commercial Building Projects" | **Recommended** |
| Related case studies | "Similar Case Studies" | **Recommended** (2 related) |
| Cost page | "Curious about pricing?" | **Recommended** |
| Request Quote | "Have a similar project?" | **Required** |

### TO case study pages, links FROM:

- Core_Service pillar pages (as "Deep Dive Case Study" feature section)
- Sub-service pages (1 relevant case study preview per sub-service page)
- Location pages (case study preview for that location)
- Industry pages (most relevant case study for that sector)
- Blog articles referencing the project type or challenge
- Resources page (case study listed as a resource)

**Placement strategy:** Case study pages should appear in the main navigation (Case Studies hub linked from header) AND be embedded contextually within service, location, and industry pages for maximum link equity flow.

## Conversion Opportunity Placement

Case study pages serve a dual purpose: authority building AND lead conversion. Place conversion elements at these key moments:

| Position in Case Study | Conversion Element | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| After client challenge section | Inline callout: "Facing a similar challenge? → Talk to our team" | Capture early converters who recognize their situation |
| After solution approach section | Trust block: "25+ years solving complex facade challenges" + ghost button "View our full capabilities" | Build confidence before final decision |
| After specifications table | "Request specifications for your project → Consultation" | Capture technical buyers in research mode |
| After results section | Primary CTA: "اطلب عرض سعر" gold button + secondary "استشارة مجانية" ghost button | Main conversion point after seeing proven results |
| Related case studies sidebar | "Browse more case studies" | Keep buyers on site, build trust further |

## SEO Differences vs. Project Pages (Summary)

The key difference in SEO value is search query alignment:

- **Project page ranks for:** "aluminum facade north Riyadh 2023" — low competition, low volume, hyper-specific
- **Case study page ranks for:** "aluminum facade contractor hotel Riyadh", "how to find curtain wall contractor Saudi Arabia", "best aluminum curtain wall company" — medium competition, medium volume, high conversion intent

Case studies therefore complement project pages: together they cover both the long-tail specific queries (project pages) and the mid-tail commercial intent queries (case studies) that no single page can serve.

## Meta Template

**Title (English):** `[Service Outcome] — [Client Type] Case Study in [Location] | Al-Fahad`
**Example:** `Aluminum Curtain Wall Facade — Commercial Tower Case Study in Riyadh | Al-Fahad Contracting`

**Title (Arabic):** `[نتيجة الخدمة] — دراسة حالة [نوع العميل] في [الموقع] | مؤسسة الفهد`
**Example:** `واجهة كيرتن وول ألمنيوم — دراسة حالة برج تجاري في الرياض | مؤسسة الفهد`

**Meta Description (English):** `How Al-Fahad delivered [scope] of [service] for a [client type] in [location]. Challenge → Solution → Results. [Key metric]. Request a quote for your project.`
**Example:** `How Al-Fahad delivered 4,800 sqm aluminum curtain wall for a commercial tower in North Riyadh. Ahead of schedule. Zero post-handover defects. Request a quote for your project.`
