## Introduction

This keyword research covers the full Saudi Arabian search landscape for aluminum, glass, and steel contracting services. Keywords are organized into 13 categories per service, spanning all buyer types: villa owners, real estate developers, general contractors, government procurement officers, architects, and engineering consultants.

**Search volume estimates** are based on regional data for Saudi Arabia (KSA), with Riyadh as the primary locale. Arabic queries dominate at approximately 75–80% of total search volume; English queries represent the expatriate professional and international developer segment.
