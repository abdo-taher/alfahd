# 21 — Competitor Analysis Framework
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the competitive intelligence framework, evaluation criteria, scoring rubric, quarterly audit template, and 90-day quick-win gap analysis for the Saudi aluminum/glass/steel contracting space.

---

## Framework Overview

Competitive intelligence in SEO is not about monitoring competitors obsessively — it is about identifying the specific content, keyword, and technical gaps where Al-Fahad can gain measurable organic advantage within 90 days. Quarterly audits ensure the strategy adapts as competitors invest in SEO.

**Competitive scope:**
- Primary: Riyadh-based aluminum, glass, and steel contractors with measurable online presence
- Secondary: Saudi-wide contractors with Riyadh market activity
- Tertiary: International brands with Saudi-targeted pages

## Evaluation Criteria Table

For each competitor, evaluate 7 dimensions on a 1–5 scale:

| Criterion | Definition | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Content Depth** | Quality and depth of service page content (word count, structure, technical detail) | Manual review + SEO crawl |
| **Keyword Coverage** | Breadth of keywords the site ranks for (especially Arabic commercial keywords) | Ahrefs / Semrush keyword gap |
| **Domain Authority** | Moz DA or Ahrefs DR score | Tool measurement |
| **Backlink Profile** | Number and quality of referring domains | Ahrefs / Majestic |
| **Technical SEO Health** | Site speed, mobile optimization, crawlability, hreflang | Google PageSpeed + Screaming Frog |
| **Local SEO Presence** | GBP completeness, review count, Local Pack rankings | Manual GBP review + BrightLocal |
| **Conversion Optimization** | Quality of CTAs, RFQ forms, trust signals, and conversion architecture | Manual review |

## Scoring Rubric: 1–5 Scale

| Score | Content Depth | Keyword Coverage | Domain Authority | Backlinks | Technical SEO | Local SEO | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5 (Excellent)** | 2,000+ words per service page, structured, bilingual, FAQ, case studies | 5,000+ Arabic keywords ranking | DA 60+ | 500+ referring domains | LCP <2.5s, mobile perfect, full hreflang | 50+ GBP reviews, top 3 Local Pack | Dedicated RFQ form, trust signals, strong CTA hierarchy |
| **4 (Good)** | 1,000–2,000 words, some structure, mostly Arabic | 1,000–5,000 Arabic keywords | DA 40–60 | 100–500 referring domains | LCP 2.5–4s, mobile OK | 20–50 reviews, Local Pack appearances | RFQ page exists, some trust signals |
| **3 (Average)** | 300–1,000 words, limited structure, some Arabic | 200–1,000 keywords | DA 25–40 | 25–100 referring domains | LCP 4–6s, some mobile issues | 5–20 reviews, inconsistent Local Pack | Contact form, minimal CTAs |
| **2 (Poor)** | <300 words, generic, English-only or poor Arabic | <200 keywords | DA 10–25 | 5–25 referring domains | LCP >6s, significant mobile issues | <5 reviews, rarely in Local Pack | Single phone number, no form |
| **1 (Absent)** | No service pages, product thumbnails only | <50 keywords | DA <10 | <5 referring domains | Not mobile-friendly, fails Core Web Vitals | Not on GBP, no reviews | No conversion mechanism |
