# 23 — SEO Roadmap
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the phased 24-month SEO execution roadmap — objectives, task lists, resource requirements, traffic/lead targets, and phase-transition metrics for each phase.

---

## Roadmap Overview and Phasing Rationale

Al-Fahad's SEO program is structured in four phases, each building on the previous:

- **Phase 1 (Months 0–3): Foundation & Technical Setup** — Zero organic presence to a properly configured, crawlable, Schema-marked bilingual site with core commercial pages published
- **Phase 2 (Months 3–6): Content Buildout & On-Page Optimization** — Core pages supplemented by full sub-service, industry, location, and blog content; topical authority begins establishing
- **Phase 3 (Months 6–12): Authority Building & Topical Dominance** — Link building, digital PR, review acquisition, AI entity establishment; competitive head terms become reachable
- **Phase 4 (Months 12–24): Scaling, GCC Expansion & AI Search Dominance** — Full Saudi market coverage, GCC market entry, AI search citation establishment, compounding growth

---
