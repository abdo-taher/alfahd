# 14 — Local SEO Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the complete local SEO strategy — Google Business Profile, review acquisition, citations, NAP consistency, and local link building — for dominating Riyadh and Saudi Arabia's Local Pack results.

---

## Strategy Overview

Local SEO is Al-Fahad's fastest route to qualified lead generation. "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض" queries trigger Google's Local Pack (the map results showing 3 businesses), which appears above all organic results and captures 25–35% of clicks. A well-optimized Google Business Profile can generate 20–50 qualified inquiries per month from Riyadh buyers alone — before the website ranks for any competitive head terms.

**The local SEO opportunity:** The Saudi aluminum/glass/steel contracting space has almost no competitors with fully optimized Google Business Profiles, systematic review acquisition, or structured citation campaigns. Al-Fahad can dominate the Local Pack within 60–90 days by executing this strategy.

## 2. Review Acquisition Strategy

### Target Review Velocity

| Platform | Month 3 Target | Month 6 Target | Month 12 Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | 15 reviews | 35 reviews | 75 reviews |
| Average star rating | 4.5+ | 4.7+ | 4.7+ |

### Post-Project Review Request Workflow

**Step 1 — Project Handover (Day 0):** Project manager presents a physical "Thank You" card with QR code linking to Google review page.

**Step 2 — WhatsApp Follow-up (Day 3):** Send WhatsApp message to client contact:

```
[Arabic WhatsApp Template]
السلام عليكم [اسم العميل]،

نشكركم على ثقتكم بمؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات في مشروع [وصف المشروع].

يسعدنا معرفة رأيكم في الخدمة المقدمة. مشاركة تقييمكم على Google تساعدنا كثيرًا في خدمة المزيد من العملاء.

رابط التقييم: [Google Review Direct Link]

شكرًا جزيلًا لكم.
فريق مؤسسة الفهد
```

**Step 3 — Email Follow-up (Day 7):** For developer/government clients with formal email:

```
[Email Template]
Subject: Feedback Request — [Project Name] | Al-Fahad Contracting

Dear [Contact Name],

Thank you for entrusting Al-Fahad Contracting with [project description]. We are pleased to have delivered [key outcome].

We would greatly appreciate a moment to share your feedback on Google — your review helps us continue delivering excellence for Saudi construction projects.

[Leave a Review → Direct Google Review Link]

Thank you for your partnership.

Al-Fahad Contracting Team
مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات
```

**Step 4 — Final Reminder (Day 21):** One final WhatsApp or email (if no review received).

### Review Response Strategy

- Respond to every review within 24 hours
- For 5-star reviews: thank client and mention a specific project detail to show authenticity
- For 4-star reviews: thank and acknowledge any constructive feedback
- For 1–3 star reviews: respond professionally, address the concern, offer offline resolution. Never argue in public responses.

## 4. NAP Consistency

**Canonical NAP Format — use this EXACT format on every platform:**

```
Name:    مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات
         Al-Fahad Contracting Company

Address: [Full Riyadh street address]
         Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
         
Phone:   +966 XX XXX XXXX

Website: https://alfahad.com/
```

**Rules:**
- Always use the full legal Arabic name: مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات (not "الفهد" alone)
- Always use the English equivalent: Al-Fahad Contracting Company (not "Al-Fahad Contracting" or "Alfahad")
- Phone number must include country code (+966)
- Website URL must use canonical form with trailing slash, HTTPS
