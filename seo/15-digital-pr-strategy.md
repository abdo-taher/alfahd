# 15 — Digital PR Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the digital PR strategy for building domain authority, brand recognition, and AI search entity establishment through earned media, industry publications, linkable assets, and professional association presence.

---

## PR Strategy Overview and Objectives

Digital PR for Al-Fahad serves three distinct SEO goals:

1. **Domain Authority Building** — Backlinks from high-DA Saudi construction, architecture, and business publications directly increase Al-Fahad's ranking power for competitive commercial keywords.
2. **Entity Establishment** — Press mentions, named citations, and brand references in authoritative contexts train Google's entity graph (and AI systems) to recognize Al-Fahad as the definitive aluminum/glass/steel contractor in Saudi Arabia.
3. **AI Search Citation** — ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity increasingly cite named companies and authoritative sources. A company with published reports, industry quotes, and media presence is dramatically more likely to be cited in AI-generated answers.

## 6. Content for Links (Linkable Asset Strategy)

The most scalable link building approach is creating assets valuable enough that other sites link to them without being asked. Target: 5 cornerstone linkable assets.

### Asset 1: Saudi Facade Industry Report 2025 (سوق الواجهات السعودي)

- **Format:** PDF + web landing page
- **Content:** Market size estimates, key sectors driving demand, material trends, Vision 2030 impact on facade sector, regional growth projections
- **Target link sources:** Construction publications, architecture schools, consulting firms, government project pages
- **Expected links:** 15–30 from DA 30–70 sources
- **Positioning:** "The first comprehensive report on the Saudi facade contracting market, published by Al-Fahad Contracting — 25 years of industry data"

### Asset 2: Vision 2030 Construction Trends Guide (دليل اتجاهات البناء في رؤية 2030)

- **Format:** Comprehensive web guide (3,000+ words) + downloadable summary
- **Content:** Vision 2030 project pipeline, construction investment by sector, facade and structural demand forecast, material specifications expected for mega-projects
- **Target link sources:** Developer websites, government contractor databases, construction trade press, university architecture departments
- **Expected links:** 10–20

### Asset 3: Aluminum vs. Steel vs. Glass: The Complete Cost Comparison Guide (مقارنة تكاليف الألمنيوم والحديد والزجاج)

- **Format:** Web page + downloadable PDF comparison chart
- **Content:** Cost per sqm by material, climate performance, lifespan, maintenance costs, application suitability
- **Target link sources:** Architecture/engineering blogs, developer resources pages, construction guides, material supplier websites
- **Expected links:** 20–40 (this type of comparison resource is heavily linked)

### Asset 4: Saudi Building Code: Facade Requirements 2025 (متطلبات الواجهات في كود البناء السعودي 2025)

- **Format:** Comprehensive web guide with linked sections
- **Content:** Annotated summary of SBC facade chapters, fire resistance requirements, wind load specifications, glazing standards
- **Target link sources:** Architecture forums, engineering consultancy websites, government project managers
- **Expected links:** 10–25 (authoritative regulatory content earns consistent links)

### Asset 5: Riyadh Neighborhood Construction Guide (دليل أحياء الرياض للبناء والتطوير)

- **Format:** Interactive/visual web guide
- **Content:** Construction activity by Riyadh district, permit data trends, recommended material specifications by zone
- **Target link sources:** Real estate developer blogs, property portals, architecture news sites, Riyadh municipal publications
- **Expected links:** 15–30
