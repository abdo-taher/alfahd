# 07 — Location SEO Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the strategy for all location-specific landing pages — Riyadh districts, Riyadh neighborhoods, and future expansion cities. Location pages capture high-intent geo-specific queries and support Local Pack rankings.

---

## Overview

Location pages allow Al-Fahad to rank for "[service] + [location]" queries across Riyadh's distinct geographic zones and future expansion markets. These pages are among the easiest to rank (very low competition, ultra-specific intent) and convert at the highest rate because they show the searcher Al-Fahad operates in their exact area.

**URL pattern:** `/en/locations/{location}/` | `/ar/manatiq-al-khidma/{location}/`

## Part 4: How Location Pages Link to Core_Services, Industries, and Projects

Every location page follows this internal linking pattern:

```
Location Page
  ↓ Links TO:
    ├── /services/aluminum/           (anchor: "Aluminum Works in [City/District]")
    ├── /services/glass/              (anchor: "Glass Facade Works in [City/District]")
    ├── /services/steel/              (anchor: "Steel Works in [City/District]")
    ├── /industries/{relevant}/       (anchor: most relevant industry for that location)
    ├── /projects/                    (anchor: "View Projects in [City/District]" — link to filtered portfolio)
    └── /request-quote/               (anchor: "Request a Quote for [City/District] Projects")

  ↑ Receives links FROM:
    ├── /locations/                   (locations hub)
    ├── Riyadh district pages link to neighborhood pages
    ├── Blog articles mentioning specific locations
    └── Project pages tagged with that location
```

**NAP Consistency Note:** All location pages must reference Al-Fahad's canonical address, phone number, and business name exactly as registered on Google Business Profile. See `14-local-seo-strategy.md` for the canonical NAP format.
