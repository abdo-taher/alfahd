---

## Content Mission Statement

Al-Fahad's content mission is to be the most comprehensive Arabic and English resource for aluminum, glass, and steel contracting knowledge in Saudi Arabia — educating developers, contractors, architects, government buyers, and villa owners at every stage of their project journey so that when they are ready to hire a contractor, Al-Fahad is the only name they trust.

Every article must serve one or more of these goals:
1. Capture a specific search query (SEO traffic)
2. Build topical authority (domain authority compounding)
3. Nurture a buyer toward an RFQ (conversion)
4. Establish Al-Fahad as a named, citable entity in AI search (AI citation)

## Content-to-Conversion Path by Funnel Stage

### TOFU (Top of Funnel — Awareness)

Visitor arrives via informational search (e.g., "ما هو الكيرتن وول").

**Path:**
Blog article → Internal link to service pillar page → Internal link to industry page → Cost guide callout → **CTA: استشارة مجانية**

**Conversion rate expectation:** 0.5–1.5% visitor-to-inquiry

### MOFU (Middle of Funnel — Consideration)

Visitor arrives via commercial search (e.g., "تكلفة واجهات الألمنيوم").

**Path:**
Cost guide page → Related service page → Project/case study gallery → Trust signals → **CTA: اطلب عرض سعر**

**Conversion rate expectation:** 1.5–3% visitor-to-inquiry

### BOFU (Bottom of Funnel — Decision)

Visitor arrives via transactional search (e.g., "مقاول ألمنيوم الرياض").

**Path:**
Service pillar page → Sub-service page → Case study → Contact/RFQ page → **CTA: اطلب عرض سعر الآن**

**Conversion rate expectation:** 3–6% visitor-to-inquiry
