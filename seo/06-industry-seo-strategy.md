# 06 — Industry SEO Strategy
## مؤسسة الفهد للمقاولات | Al-Fahad Contracting Company

> **Document Purpose:** Define the SEO strategy for each of the 10 industry vertical landing pages. These pages capture B2B buyers who search by project type rather than by service name — bridging the gap between "what we offer" and "who we serve."

---

## Overview

Industry pages serve two functions simultaneously: they capture high-intent searches from buyers in specific sectors (e.g., "aluminum contractor for hotels"), and they signal to Google that Al-Fahad has deep expertise across multiple verticals. Each page acts as a spoke linking to Core_Service pillar pages, relevant project pages, and the RFQ page.

**URL pattern:** `/en/industries/{vertical}/` | `/ar/qita3at/{vertical}/`

---

**URL:** `/en/industries/villas/` | `/ar/qita3at/fiyyal/`

|---|---|---|---|
| أعمال ألمنيوم للفلل الرياض | Aluminum works for villas Riyadh | Commercial | High |
| نوافذ وأبواب ألمنيوم للفلل | Aluminum windows and doors for villas | Transactional | High |
| واجهات ألمنيوم وزجاج للفلل الفاخرة | Aluminum and glass facades for luxury villas | Commercial | High |

| أسعار ألمنيوم الفلل الرياض | Villa aluminum prices Riyadh | Commercial | High |

### Search Intent

Villa owners and residential developers seeking complete aluminum and glass packages for new-build or renovation projects. Query patterns: "مقاول ألمنيوم فلل الرياض" and "نوافذ ألمنيوم فلل". Decision cycle: 1–4 weeks.

### Recommended Landing Page URL

`/en/industries/villas/` | `/ar/qita3at/fiyyal/`

### Content Strategy

- Open with villa-specific pain points: matching architectural style, premium finishes, budget-conscious installation
- Gallery: 4–6 villa project photos (North Riyadh neighborhoods preferred)
- Service grid: Windows, Doors, Facades, Skylights, Custom Systems
- Trust: "We've completed 50+ villa projects in Riyadh"
- Cost callout: link to aluminum windows and doors cost pages
- Recommended word count: 800–1,200 words

### Internal Linking Strategy

- → `/en/services/aluminum/windows/` — "Aluminum Windows for Villas"
- → `/en/services/aluminum/doors/` — "Aluminum Doors"
- → `/en/services/aluminum/facades/` — "Villa Facade Systems"
- → `/en/locations/north-riyadh/` — "Projects in North Riyadh"
- → `/en/cost/aluminum-windows/` — "Window Cost Guide"
- → `/en/request-quote/` — "Request a Quote"

### Primary Conversion Goal

RFQ form submission — "اطلب عرض سعر لمشروع فلتك"

### How This Page Links to Core_Services and Projects

Each service card links directly to the relevant Core_Service pillar. The project gallery links to individual project pages tagged with the "villas" category. This creates a tri-directional equity flow: industry page ↔ service pages ↔ project pages.
